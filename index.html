The AI bridge on this computer

Run this command in a terminal. It connects this page to the AI tools on your computer: Ollama, Claude Code and Codex. 

npx https://aakin21.github.io/2D-Battle-Simulation/ai-bridge.tgz

The first time, the browser asks whether this site may reach your computer: allow it.

Needs Node.js 20.12 or newer. Gemini too: put GEMINI_API_KEY=… in .env.local in the folder you run it from.

The browser blocks this site from reaching apps on this device. Allow it again in the site settings (the icon left of the address bar): "Local network access", or "Loopback network" in newer versions. Then press Try again.

About

A thesis project: a 2D battle simulation in which every soldier follows a few rules of courage, movement and combat, so that armies fight, flee and regroup on their own.

The heroes who lead them can be commanded by you, by built-in rules, or by AI: a large language model, alone or with a fast decision model for each hero.

Art, sounds and font: Ninja Adventure Asset Pack by Pixel-boy (CC0).
